<p><span><strong>Scholarship</strong></span></p>
<p><span><strong>Serving Others Worldwide started the </strong></span><span><strong>Scholarship Program</strong></span><span><strong>, allowing each donation to benefit the scholarship receiver directly. To fulfill the financial assistance gap in the education system in China, we have collaborated with a Hong Kong charity to assist approved students so they may finish high school education and obtain a chance at higher education to move away from poverty.</strong></span></p>
<p><strong>Program Time Length</strong><span> :<span><strong>Each scholarship will help a student through 3 years of high school education that starts in September of every year.</strong></span></span></p>
<p><strong>Program Targets: </strong><span><strong>The student must hold a place within the first 800 place out of a county of 20,000 students for the national high school entrance exam. Besides the academic requirement, the student must also fulfill one of the requirements below:</strong></span></p>
<ul>
<li><span><strong>Orphan</strong></span></li>
<li><span><strong>One of the parents is deceased or disabled without ability to work</strong></span></li>
<li><span><strong>Children of families receiving the minimum social care</strong></span></li>
<li><span><strong>Whose guardian is a designated social welfare organization</strong></span></li>
<li><span><strong>Other circumstances resulting in a verified low-income household</strong></span></li>
</ul>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong> Scholarship Amount: </strong><span><strong>Approximately US$1,800 to support their tuition, room and board, textbooks, and other miscellaneous expenses.</strong></span></p>
<p><strong>Payout Timeframe: </strong><span><strong>30 monthly payments, 10 per year for 3 years. Each installment support amount is US$60, totaling $1,800 after 3 years.</strong></span></p>
<p><strong> Progress Monitoring: </strong><span><strong>S.O.W. will continue to be in touch and follow up with each scholarship recipients. We will also organize trips for the donors to visit the school to understand the progress of their donation.</strong></span></p>
