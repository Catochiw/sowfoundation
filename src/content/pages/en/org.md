<p><strong>About Us:</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: left;"><img src="/images/20180213/20180213060431_43859.jpg" alt="" /></p>
<p style="text-align: left;"><span><span><strong>S.O.W.</strong></span>, or Serving Others Worldwide, is a nonprofit organization incorporated in the San Francisco Bay Area in 2016. The team of S.O.W. strives to serve our communities by providing programs, projects or tangible assistance to meet their economic, physical, mental and spiritual needs on local and global level.</span></p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>Vision:</strong>&nbsp; Let the most needy see the love of God through our actions.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>Mission:</strong>&nbsp; To serve our communities worldwide by providing programs, projects or tangible assistance to meet their economic, physical, mental and spiritual needs.</p>
