S.O.W. is led by a volunteer board with decades of combined experience across pastoral ministry, business, philanthropy, and grassroots service.

## Rev. Thomas Chan

**Chairman of the Board**

![Rev. Thomas Chan](https://peggychandesign.files.wordpress.com/2020/02/3i3a0024.jpg?w=392&h=595&zoom=2)

"I'm just a passenger, a speck of dust — my name doesn't matter. I hope this speck of dust will be a blessing to others on the earth, and that it will honour the name of the Lord."

Pastor Chan became a believer in middle school. In 2009 he was called by God, resigned from his career as a director at APL, and entered the Overseas Theological Seminary in the United States, completing a three-year theological curriculum. In 2012 God arranged for him to serve as a hospice pastor for five years. In 2016 he was moved, alongside three other directors, to establish Serving Others Worldwide (S.O.W.) to help those in need. He was ordained in 2019.

## Rev. Esther Lou

**Director**

![Rev. Esther Lou](https://peggychandesign.files.wordpress.com/2020/02/3i3a9962.jpg?w=398&h=578&zoom=2)

Rev. Esther Lou came from Taiwan and graduated from Fu Jen Catholic University with a Bachelor's in History. She and her husband worked in the U.S. restaurant industry for 35 years, running five restaurants at one point. She was also a successful real-estate agent. Later she turned her focus to her faith and earned a Master of Christian Ministry from Christian Witness Theological Seminary.

Through a season of crisis — her husband's struggle with addiction — God restored their marriage and directed them to serve together in the mission field.

Rev. Lou is the founder of Herald Restaurant Gospel Ministries and currently serves as President and Executive Director of World Christian Restaurant Ministries. She is also a member of the executive boards of Operation Dawn USA and Serving Others Worldwide (which she co-founded in 2017). Her books include *學開餐廳四十招*, *笑口查經樂*, *五滋六味傳福音*, and *全球福音爆炸 — 餐福事工策略*, along with video curricula such as "ABCD" and "Life 168" used to train life teachers. Rev. Lou and her husband have two children and four grandchildren.

## Mr. Sau-Wing Lam

**Director**

![Mr. Sau-Wing Lam](https://peggychandesign.files.wordpress.com/2020/02/maxresdefault.jpg)

Sau-Wing was born and raised in a Christian family in Hong Kong. He came to faith at age 12, met his wife Edith at 16, and came to the U.S. as a foreign student at 17. He earned an MBA from U.C. Berkeley and went on to senior executive positions in banking, finance and telecommunications. His last role was Chief Executive Officer and Chairman of the Board of United Commercial Bank, then the largest bank serving the Chinese community in the U.S. At 45, by God's grace, he left the commercial world to devote himself full-time to financial-stewardship ministry. For the past 20 years he has used financial management as a tool to reach the unsaved with the gospel — remaining an unpaid volunteer throughout.

Each week he hosts five live financial radio programmes in San Francisco, Los Angeles and New York, answering call-in questions from listeners. Each Friday evening he hosts a live television programme on KTSF. He also answers an average of 100 e-mails a day from listeners across the country, and visits families with special needs to help them solve their problems.

Sau-Wing recently completed a seven-year tenure as Ministry Director of the Northern California branch of Chinese Christian Herald Crusades and Chief Editor of the Northwestern edition of *Herald Monthly* — both unpaid roles. He has led 16 tours to Jordan and Israel. Beginning in the second half of 2018, he started leading "Ark Tours," bringing groups to Kentucky to visit the Creation Museum and Ark Encounter and teach about Creation and the global flood.

## Ms. Sue Chan

**Director**

![Ms. Sue Chan](https://peggychandesign.files.wordpress.com/2020/02/3i3a0050-3.jpg?w=396&h=595&zoom=2)

Sue Chan is a volunteer and one of the founding board members of Serving Others Worldwide (S.O.W.). She earned a Master of Science from Purdue University and worked for the telecommunications company Sprint for 15 years. She currently serves as Deputy Executive Director of World Christian Restaurant Ministries.
