<p><strong>About Us:</strong></p>
<p><img src="/images/20180213/20180213053955_13683.jpg" alt="" /></p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><span><span><strong>S.O.W.</strong></span><span><strong>, or Serving Others Worldwide, is a nonprofit organization incorporated in the San Francisco Bay Area in 2016. The team of S.O.W. strives to serve our communities by providing programs, projects or tangible assistance to meet their economic, physical, mental and spiritual needs on local and global level.</strong></span><br /> <span><br /> </span></span></p>
<p><span><span><strong>Vision: &nbsp;Let the most needy see the love of God through our actions.</strong></span></span></p>
<p><strong><br /> </strong></p>
<p><strong>Mission: &nbsp;To serve our communities worldwide by providing programs, projects or tangible assistance to meet their economic, physical, mental and spiritual needs. </strong></p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>[fixedimg url="/demo/assets/images/homepage/2.jpg" title="Disaster Relief" title-position="right"]</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong><span>Disaster Relief<br /> S.O.W. Disaster Relief aims to unite all churches together to provide relief efforts and resources to victims and the affected people, assisting them in reconstruction.<br /> When a disaster strikes, S.O.W. will integrate the resources of all churches and Christians, including personnel and tangible supplies, to work with local churches in the affected areas to play an active role in disaster relief in their communities and to exalt the Holy Cross and glorify the Lord&rsquo;s name. <br /> </span></strong></p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>[fixedimg url="/demo/assets/images/homepage/1.jpg" title="Senior Care" title-position="left"]</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><span><strong>Senior Care</strong></span></p>
<p><span><strong> S.O.W. focuses on the elderly people and takes actions to help them solve the difficulties in their daily life. Our program also extends into Senior Home Maintenance, where we aim to help elders with their plumbing, electrical and maintenance issues around their home. S.O.W. will send experienced repair people to the homes of the elderly and provide genuine repairs to help them solve their daily inconveniences. Repairs can include all plumbing, electrical, doors, windows, kitchen, bathroom, etc. All services are provided free of charge.</strong></span></p>
<p><br /> [fixedimg url="/demo/assets/images/homepage/4.jpg" title="Study - Abroad Tours" title-position="right"]</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><span><strong>Study &ndash; Abroad Tours</strong></span><br /> <span><strong>S.O.W. plans and arranges Holy Land tours, spiritual retreats, mission trips and other thematic study-abroad tours. Through field trips and real-time lectures, incorporating theory with practice, participants can learn and have fun with interactive teaching methods to enhance their understanding of Christian faith, its history and culture. There will be a positive and far-reaching impact on their spiritual life as well.</strong></span></p>
<p>[fixedimg url="/demo/assets/images/homepage/3.jpg" title="Scholarship" title-position="left"]</p>
<p><span><strong><br /> </strong></span></p>
<p><span><strong>Scholarship </strong></span></p>
<p><span><strong>S.O.W. created a scholarship program to allow each charitable donations reach their target students fully and directly. </strong></span></p>
<p><span><strong>Scholarship Program aims at caring for and assisting poor students, who maintain a certain level of academic grades, by subsidizing for their tuition fees, room and board, textbooks, and other miscellaneous fees to help them complete their studies and degree programs. </strong></span></p>
<p><span><strong>The scholarship recipients will have an opportunity to receive education and leave poverty. S.O.W. will continue to follow up and monitor the progress of the program as well as arrange trips for donors to visit the schools and get to know each scholarship recipients.</strong></span></p>
<p><br /> [fixedimg url="/demo/assets/images/homepage/8.jpg" title="Grants" title-position="left"]</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><span><strong>Grants</strong></span><br /> <span><strong>Use practical actions to help those who need it the most. S.O.W. Grants program aims to support and care for the poor families or individuals who have encountered difficulties. During their particular difficult time, S.O.W. will maintain their necessities in life by donating funds, materials, and other means to help them be able to return to their normal life.</strong></span></p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
