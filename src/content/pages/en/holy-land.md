Study – Abroad ToursS.O.W. plans and arranges Holy Land tours, spiritual retreats, mission trips and other thematic study-abroad tours. Through field trips and real-time lectures, incorporating theory with practice, participants can learn and have fun with interactive teaching methods to enhance their understanding of Christian faith, its history and culture. There will be a positive and far-reaching impact on their spiritual life as well.Holy Land toursThrough actual field studies and a guide from biblical scholars, SOW Holy Land Tour turns the biblical stories, characters, and geography into living 3-dimensional form and helps you to break through the traditional way of studying the bible on paper. For Christians, following the footsteps of Jesus to revisit the Holy Land and reminiscing Jesus obeying the command of our Father to travel through the land to spread the Gospel and deliver salvation to us will help us to deepen our understanding of our Lord and ourselves.The itinerary of the tour includes a guide to revisit the holy places, apostle’s travels, and to witness the history of early churches. Participants will be able to come in contact with the “land,” “events,” and “things” of historical importance in the Bible. The purpose of the tour is to increase participants’ knowledge of the Bible, to connect the Bible and its geography to their life, and to strengthen their faith in Christianity.  Experiencing the Holy Land will have a profound impact on one’s spiritual life and allows one to understand the Great Commission of God, igniting the passion for mission work.All staff members serving on this tour are volunteers without any forms of compensation. They serve wholeheartedly and lead by example to help brothers and sisters to further their faith in Christianity and affirm the truth in Jesus Christ.
