The "Ark Journey" is an on-site Old Testament tour organised by S.O.W. and led by Brother Sau-Wing Lam, who teaches throughout the trip. By visiting biblical locations such as the Ark Encounter and Exodus routes, participants come to know God's story of redemption from a geographical and historical perspective.

## What makes it different

- Volunteer-led — leaders take no honorarium
- A biblical scholar accompanies the tour to teach
- Scripture and field experience interwoven for a deeper understanding of the faith

## How to join

If you'd like to be notified about the next Ark Journey, please [get in touch](/en/contact) with S.O.W. or call (510) 256-5156.
