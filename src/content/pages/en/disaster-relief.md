<p><span><span><strong>Disaster Relief</strong></span></span></p>
<p><span><strong>Serving Others Worldwide Disaster Relief Alliance aims to unite all churches to assist and to provide resources to rebuild to the victims of each disaster.</strong></span></p>
<p><strong>Traditional Mode of Disaster Relief</strong><br /> <span><strong>When a disaster strikes, the local churches and pastors often are willing to help but are not equipped with the knowledge of how to.</strong></span><br /> <span><strong>At the same time, non-local churches and their congregations also want to extend the helping hand. Traditionally there are two ways for these churches to assist the relief effort: organize fundraisers and provide the funding to known churches in the disaster zone, or they may provide the funding to a non-Christian organization to aid in the relief effort.</strong></span></p>
<p><strong>Disaster Relief with Serving Others Worldwide</strong></p>
<ol>
<li><span><strong>We will reach out to the churches within the disaster zone and ensure that the fund arrives the disaster zone within 5 days of the disaster.</strong></span></li>
<li><span><strong>Instant notification to all partnering churches within our alliance regarding the most needed supplies for each disaster zone.</strong></span></li>
<li><span><strong>Connect all resources and tangible goods from volunteers and donors.</strong></span></li>
</ol>
<p><span><strong>Serving Others Worldwide Disaster Relief Alliance centralizes the resources from each church and Christians, allowing each relief efforts to create maximum impact in each disaster. Glory to the Lord!</strong></span></p>
