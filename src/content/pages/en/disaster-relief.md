Disaster ReliefServing Others Worldwide Disaster Relief Alliance aims to unite all churches to assist and to provide resources to rebuild to the victims of each disaster.Traditional Mode of Disaster ReliefWhen a disaster strikes, the local churches and pastors often are willing to help but are not equipped with the knowledge of how to.At the same time, non-local churches and their congregations also want to extend the helping hand. Traditionally there are two ways for these churches to assist the relief effort: organize fundraisers and provide the funding to known churches in the disaster zone, or they may provide the funding to a non-Christian organization to aid in the relief effort.Disaster Relief with Serving Others Worldwide

1. **We will reach out to the churches within the disaster zone and ensure that the fund arrives the disaster zone within 5 days of the disaster.**
2. **Instant notification to all partnering churches within our alliance regarding the most needed supplies for each disaster zone.**
3. **Connect all resources and tangible goods from volunteers and donors.Serving Others Worldwide Disaster Relief Alliance centralizes the resources from each church and Christians, allowing each relief efforts to create maximum impact in each disaster.
